# Isle of Bears for the FM-1

HEYBYE GAMING's two-player stacking card game on the M-VAVE FM-1: you, the black bears, against the computer.

- [Install it](install/) (Chrome or Edge, the FM-1 plugged in over USB), version {{VERSION}}
- [Play it in the browser](emu/), no FM-1 needed: the arrows are the pad, Enter is F5, Backspace is G5
- [The rulebook](https://isle-of-bears.heybyedesign.com/rules-en.pdf)
- [Source](https://github.com/richardvt/isle-of-bears-fm1)

Installing replaces the FM-1's firmware, at your own risk. The installer can put M-VAVE's own firmware back.
